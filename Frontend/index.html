How it works

Three steps. No setup.

1

Create an account

Register with your email and log in. Your workspace is empty and private.

2

Add your applications

Save company, role, stage and notes. Paste the job description to unlock the resume match.

3

Follow up and improve

Check the dashboard daily, tick off follow-ups and close skill gaps shown by the match score.

Know your fit before you apply

Upload your resume once. For any job, the app compares the job description with your resume and shows what matches and what is missing.

  • Understands meaning, not just exact words
  • Lists the skills the job asks for that your resume lacks
  • Runs on the server with a local model, no third-party AI service
